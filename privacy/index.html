Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Who we are

Marlo ("we", "us") is building a supplement concierge service. Contact: privacy@marlo.me.

What we collect

When you apply for our beta program — via our website or via a lead form on Facebook/Instagram — we collect:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number
  • Eligibility info: country of residence, phone type, approximate daily supplement count
  • Survey answers you provide: age range, health goals, current supplements, and any other answers you choose to give

How we use it

  • To evaluate your beta application and contact you about it
  • To operate and improve the Marlo beta program
  • To send program-related communications (application status, scheduling)

We do not sell your personal information.

Lead ads

If you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares the information you entered with us under Meta's Lead Ads terms. Meta's own data practices are governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Application data is kept for the duration of the beta program and 24 months afterward, then deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting privacy@marlo.me.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy at this URL.
